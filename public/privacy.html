Privacy

Last updated October 22, 2026. You must be 18 or older to use Jules.

The short version

  • We use what you share to run Jules for you and to make Jules better.
  • We never sell it, use it for ads, or share it for other companies’ marketing.
  • Gmail is optional and read-only. Jules can’t send, change or delete email.
  • Reply STOP any time to stop texts.

Who we are

Jules is run by Jack Laylin, in New York. When this policy says “we”, it means Jack and anyone working on Jules with him. Contact: hello@textjules.com.

What we collect

  • Signup: your name, birthday, gender selection, phone number, the consent you agreed to and when. We also keep a scrambled (hashed) version of your internet connection’s address to stop repeated signup attempts. We can’t turn it back into the address.
  • How you found us: the website that sent you to signup and any campaign tag on the link you followed (for example, chatgpt.com). Never the full web address. If a friend’s shared link brought you here, we note whose link it was.
  • Messages: the texts, photos and screenshots you send Jules, Jules’s replies, and your reactions to them (like a 👍 or 👎).
  • What you tell Jules: sizes, brands, stores, likes and dislikes, your wishlist, price alerts, and photos of outfits you send so Jules can learn your style.
  • Purchases you show Jules: order screenshots you text, and what Jules reads from them (store, items, sizes, prices, dates). Jules leaves out names, addresses, contact details and card numbers.
  • Taps on links: when you tap a product link Jules sent, we record which one, so we know which suggestions were useful.
  • Gmail, if you connect it: Jules reads shopping emails (receipts, orders, shipping and returns) and their receipt PDFs. Google gives Jules read-only access; Jules can’t send, change or delete email. Jules saves short quotes from those emails as evidence, along with what it learns, like items bought, brands, stores, sizes and prices. It also keeps small details about each shopping email (date and sender’s website) so it doesn’t read the same email twice. It does not keep full email text. It may save the product photo from an order email.
  • Shop, if you connect it: your Shop order records. Jules can’t buy anything, use your saved cards or change your Shop account.

Jules never asks for or stores your card details or your passwords for stores.

How we use it

  • To run Jules for you: understand your messages and photos, find products, learn your taste and sizes, keep your wishlist and send the texts you asked for.
  • To make Jules better: the Jules team reviews conversations, feedback and purchase history to fix mistakes, and tests changes to Jules against past conversations before releasing them. We don’t sell this data or let other companies train their AI models on it.
  • To understand what’s working: which links bring people to Jules and which suggestions people tap.

Your birthday confirms you’re 18 or older. Your gender selection tells Jules which department to search (for example, women’s or men’s) until you tell it otherwise. Neither sets your sizes.

Who processes it

These companies handle your information only to provide their service to Jules:

  • OpenAI analyzes messages, photos, and the text of shopping emails and orders. OpenAI does not use this data to train its models. It may keep it for up to 30 days to check for abuse, then deletes it.
  • SerpAPI runs Jules’s web, Google Shopping and Google Lens searches. When you send a photo, a cropped picture of the item (not the whole photo) goes to Google Lens through SerpAPI to find matches.
  • Photon delivers iMessages.
  • Supabase stores your account, messages and saved data.
  • Vercel hosts the website and runs Jules.
  • Google provides Gmail access when you connect it. Shopify provides Shop access when you connect it.

Your account and saved data are stored in Canada (Montreal), where Jules also runs. OpenAI, SerpAPI and Photon process what they handle in the United States.

Who can see it

The Jules team can see your conversation and your imported purchase history, including the short email quotes behind each item, to fix problems and improve Jules. No one else can, except:

  • People you share your style report with: your style report is private until you share it. When you do, anyone with the link can see the cards you share, without signing in. That can include your first name, brands you’ve bought, totals and counts from your purchase history, the time of your last order, and an outfit photo you sent if your report uses one. Lines and cards you hide, returns, and cards you marked as ones you won’t share are left out. The link keeps working until you ask us to turn it off at hello@textjules.com.
  • When the law requires it: we’ll share information if a valid legal request requires it, or to protect someone’s safety.
  • If Jules changes hands: if Jules is sold or merged with another company, your information would move with it under this policy, and we’d tell you first.

We never sell your information, use it for ads, or share it for other companies’ marketing. We don’t share your phone number or your consent to receive texts with anyone for their marketing.

Google user data

Jules’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide and improve Jules’s shopping features for you. It is not used for ads, not sold, and not used to train general-purpose AI models. People on the Jules team read it only with your agreement (given when you connect), to fix problems, for security, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information until you delete it or close your account.

  • Deleting imported history removes everything learned from Gmail, Shop and order screenshots right away.
  • When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it within 30 days. Copies in our backups are erased within 30 days after that.
  • If Jules shuts down, we’ll text you first and delete your information within 30 days.

Keeping it safe

Your data is stored with encryption, and Gmail and Shop sign-ins are encrypted separately. Only the Jules team can open your account’s data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we’ll tell you as the law requires.

Your choices

  • Stop texts: reply STOP. Reply START to turn them back on.
  • Disconnect Gmail or Shop: on your Account page. You can also remove Jules in your Google account settings or Shop’s settings.
  • Delete imported history: on your Account page. This removes everything learned from Gmail, Shop and order screenshots.
  • See, fix or delete your information: email hello@textjules.com. We’ll reply within 30 days. You can ask for a copy of what we have, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account, messages and saved data.
  • Forget something: text Jules to forget a size, brand or other detail, and it’s removed from your profile.

Children

Jules is only for people 18 or older. If we learn someone under 18 signed up, we delete their account.

Changes to this policy

If we make an important change, we’ll text you before it applies. The date at the top shows the last change.
