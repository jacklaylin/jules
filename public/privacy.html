Privacy

Last updated [date]. Jules is a small invite-only test with up to ten people. You must be 18 or older to use Jules.

The short version

  • We use what you share only to run Jules for you.
  • We never sell it, use it for ads, or share it for other companies’ marketing.
  • Gmail is optional and read-only. Jules can’t send, change or delete email.
  • Reply STOP any time to stop texts.

What we collect

  • Signup: your name, birthday, gender selection, phone number, the consent you agreed to and when.
  • How you found us: the website that sent you to signup and any campaign tag on the link you followed (for example, chatgpt.com). Never the full web address.
  • Messages: the texts and photos you send Jules and Jules’s replies.
  • What you tell Jules: sizes, brands, stores, likes and dislikes, your wishlist and price alerts.
  • Gmail, if you connect it: Jules reads shopping emails (receipts, orders, shipping and returns) and their receipt PDFs. Google gives Jules read-only access; Jules can’t send, change or delete email. Jules saves short quotes from those emails as evidence, along with what it learns, like items bought, brands, stores and sizes. It does not keep full email text.
  • Shop, if you connect it: your Shop order records. Jules can’t buy anything, use your saved cards or change your Shop account.

How we use it

Only to run Jules for you: understand your messages, find products, learn your taste and sizes, keep your wishlist and send texts you asked for. Birthday and gender are account details; they don’t set your sizes or style. How you found us tells us which links bring people to Jules.

Who processes it

  • OpenAI analyzes messages, photos, and the text of shopping emails and orders. Jules uses OpenAI’s API with storage turned off for these requests; OpenAI does not use API data to train its models.
  • Photon delivers iMessages.
  • Supabase stores your account, messages and saved data.
  • Vercel hosts the website and runs Jules.

Who can see it

During this test, the Jules team can see your conversation and your imported purchase history, including the short email quotes behind each item, to fix problems and improve how Jules reads receipts. No one else can.

We never sell your information, use it for ads, or share it for other companies’ marketing.

Google user data

Jules’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide Jules’s shopping features to you. It is not used for ads, not sold, and not used to train general-purpose AI models.

How long we keep it

We keep your information until you delete it or close your account. Deleting imported history removes everything learned from Gmail and Shop right away. [Founder to confirm: what happens to data when the test ends.]

Your choices

  • Stop texts: reply STOP. Reply START to turn them back on.
  • Disconnect Gmail or Shop: on your Account page. You can also remove Jules in your Google account settings or Shop’s settings.
  • Delete imported history: on your Account page. This removes everything learned from Gmail and Shop.
  • Delete everything: email hello@textjules.com and we’ll delete your account, messages and saved data.
