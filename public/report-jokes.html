What these numbers mean
Reports built
First reports built in the period; a rebuild doesn't count again. Higher is better.
Opened
Testers who opened their own report (your admin preview doesn't count). Visits count at most once a day per report.
Started a share
Testers who tapped to share by any method: copy link, Instagram Story, the phone's share sheet or a download. A tap is not a post: neither the phone nor Instagram tells a web page whether a share went out, so a cancelled or unposted share still counts.
Friends opened
First opens of a shared link per browser: the proof a share actually went out. A Story counts here only when it had the link sticker and someone tapped it. Bots, link previews and the owner's own browser are left out.
Signups
People who signed up through a tester's link. The report and the style card share one link code, so this counts both.
Votes up
Share of thumbs up among the latest vote per card per report, cast in the period. Changes in points.
Not tracked
Opens, share taps and friends' opens are counted from October 22, 2026; cards reached, card images and the share method from that evening. Earlier periods say New, never 0.
