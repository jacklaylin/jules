How to avoid counting costs twice

Record variable charges in daily usage. Record fixed plan fees in monthly subscription, once per service per month. Don’t record a credit top-up and the consumption of those same credits as two expenses. Include credit consumption in daily usage only when it is not already covered by your subscription; otherwise record $0 and put the credit count in the note. Credit notes are informational and are not converted to dollars.

Subscriptions here are an allocation of the monthly fee, not the cash payment date. Taxes, refunds, annual plans, and shared accounts need manual adjustments to the monthly amount. Include only the portion you want assigned to Jules. Set a provider spending limit wherever available; this dashboard cannot enforce one.
