How to avoid counting costs twice

Record variable charges in daily usage. Record fixed plan fees (Photon lines, Supabase, ChatGPT, Claude) once as a monthly subscription: it carries forward to later months until you enter a new amount, or 0 when a plan stops. Where a connected provider reports its own plan fee (SerpApi, Vercel), the page uses that and ignores a manual subscription; where OpenAI reports a day, its billed figure replaces any manual or estimated amount. Don’t record a credit top-up and the consumption of those same credits as two expenses. Include credit consumption in daily usage only when it is not already covered by your subscription; otherwise record $0 and put the credit count in the note. Credit notes are informational and are not converted to dollars.

Subscriptions here are an allocation of the monthly fee, not the cash payment date. Taxes, refunds, annual plans, and shared accounts need manual adjustments to the monthly amount. Include only the portion you want assigned to Jules. Set a provider spending limit wherever available; this dashboard cannot enforce one.

ChatGPT / Codex and Claude are development expenses. The OpenAI API is recorded separately. GitHub includes any paid repository tools.

Billed = the provider’s own reported dollars. Estimate = Jules’ own count of its calls at list prices, used only where billing isn’t available yet. Manual = entered with Update an amount. The status line compares OpenAI’s billed dollars with Jules’ own recorded calls for the days OpenAI has finished reporting; a gap means some calls are made outside the cost meter. Plan fees are spread evenly over the month. Projected month end is calculated, not reported by any provider: the month’s fees plus the average daily usage so far, times the days in the month. It is a forecast, not a bill. Missing entries are unknown, not free. Provider reporting can lag by a day or two; this page cannot enforce spending caps.

Each person’s metered OpenAI and SerpApi spend for today and this month (UTC), priced at today’s list prices, against their spending cap. It always shows the current day and month, whatever date is picked. Caps are set in Vercel (COST-CAPS.md); with none set, nobody is limited. Photon, Vercel and Supabase fees are shared and not split per person.

Every scheduled job Jules runs, from lib/jobs.js. A test fails if one is scheduled without being listed, and Supabase jobs not listed show here as untracked. Costs are estimates of the OpenAI and SerpApi calls each job makes (also counted By feature); compute time is in the Vercel and Supabase rows, which their billing does not split per job. Projected is the month so far averaged per day, times the days in the month. Set a job’s monthly budget with Update an amount; a job is flagged when it is over or projected over budget, when a day costs more than double its daily average, or when it stops running.
