What these numbers mean
Month to date
Every service’s dollars from the 1st through the picked day (UTC). Plan fees are spread evenly over the month. Missing entries are unknown, not free, so an incomplete service makes it the known part only.
Projected month end
Calculated, not reported by any provider: the month’s fees plus the average daily usage so far, times the days in the month. It is a forecast, not a bill. It is compared with last month’s whole total; lower is better.
Today / Day
That day’s known spend against the daily warning budget (set with Update an amount). This page warns; it cannot enforce a cap. Set a provider spending limit wherever available.
Lifetime
Every whole month since the build started plus this month to date. A month missing a service counts only what is known, and the status line names it.
Billed
The provider’s own reported dollars.
Estimate
Jules’ own count of its calls at list prices, used only where billing isn’t available yet. OpenAI calls are priced from token counts; SerpApi searches as a share of the plan price.
Manual
Entered with Update an amount.
Billed vs recorded
The status line compares OpenAI’s billed dollars with Jules’ own recorded calls for the days OpenAI has finished reporting; a gap means some calls are made outside the cost meter. Provider reporting can lag by a day or two.
By feature
Estimates from every OpenAI and SerpApi call Jules makes, split by what it was for. Eval test runs are under Evals. Billed totals (By service) are the source of truth.
By person
Each person’s metered OpenAI and SerpApi spend for today and this month (UTC), at today’s list prices, against their spending cap. It always shows the current day and month, whatever date is picked. Caps are set in Vercel (COST-CAPS.md); with none set, nobody is limited. Photon, Vercel and Supabase fees are shared and not split per person.
Recurring jobs
Every scheduled job from lib/jobs.js; a test fails if one is scheduled without being listed, and Supabase jobs not listed show as untracked. Costs are estimates of each job’s OpenAI and SerpApi calls (also counted By feature); compute time is in the Vercel and Supabase rows. Projected is the month so far averaged per day, times the days in the month. A job is flagged when it is over or projected over its monthly budget, when a day costs more than double its daily average, or when it stops running.
Evals
Owner test runs from the replay gate, estimated the same way. They are not user activity.

Update an amount

Each save replaces that service’s amount for the selected day or month. Check provider billing before recording a value. Missing entries are unknown, not free; enter 0 to confirm no charge.

How to avoid counting costs twice

Record variable charges in daily usage. Record fixed plan fees (Photon lines, Supabase, ChatGPT, Claude) once as a monthly subscription: it carries forward to later months until you enter a new amount, or 0 when a plan stops. Where a connected provider reports its own plan fee (SerpApi, Vercel), the page uses that and ignores a manual subscription; where OpenAI reports a day, its billed figure replaces any manual or estimated amount. Don’t record a credit top-up and the consumption of those same credits as two expenses. Include credit consumption in daily usage only when it is not already covered by your subscription; otherwise record $0 and put the credit count in the note. Credit notes are informational and are not converted to dollars.

Subscriptions here are an allocation of the monthly fee, not the cash payment date. Taxes, refunds, annual plans, and shared accounts need manual adjustments to the monthly amount. Include only the portion you want assigned to Jules. Set a provider spending limit wherever available; this dashboard cannot enforce one.

ChatGPT / Codex and Claude are development expenses. The OpenAI API is recorded separately. GitHub includes any paid repository tools.
