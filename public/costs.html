How to avoid counting costs twice

Record variable charges in daily usage and fixed plan fees in monthly subscription, once per service per month. Where a provider is connected and reports its own plan fee (SerpApi, Vercel), the page uses the provider’s figure and ignores a manual subscription. Where OpenAI reports a day, its billed figure replaces any manual or estimated amount for that day.

Subscriptions here are an allocation of the monthly fee, not the cash-payment date. Taxes, refunds, annual plans and shared accounts need manual adjustments. ChatGPT / Codex is a development expense; the OpenAI API is recorded separately. Set a provider spending limit wherever available; this page cannot enforce one.
