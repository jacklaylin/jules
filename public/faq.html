FAQ

Updated October 2026.

What is Jules?

Jules is a personal shopper you text by iMessage, for anyone who’d rather text a screenshot than hunt through tabs. Send a screenshot, photo or link to something you want to wear, and Jules tells you what it is and where to get it in your size, including cheaper options and resale. Jules can watch the price and text you when it drops or your size is back in stock. You buy on the store’s own site. As of October 2026, Jules is in early access, free and iPhone only.

How does it work?

Text Jules from your iPhone like you’d text a friend. Send a screenshot from Instagram or TikTok, a photo you took, or a product link, and say what you’re after: the same thing in your size, something cheaper, or ideas like it. Jules replies in the same thread with a few options and a link to each store. Reply to say what you like or don’t, ask follow-up questions, or ask Jules to keep an eye on something.

What can Jules do?

  • Identify clothing, shoes, bags and accessories from a screenshot, photo or link. When it can’t confirm the exact item, it says so and shows similar options.
  • Find the item in your size at different stores, including cheaper listings and resale (pre-owned) marketplaces.
  • Suggest similar pieces for less when you want a dupe.
  • Watch prices and restocks on items you choose, and text you when the price drops, your size comes back, or it’s nearly sold out in your size.
  • Remember your sizes and the stores you like, from your texts and, if you connect Gmail, from your order receipts.
  • Show prices and shipping as the store lists them. Jules doesn’t guess prices, stock or store policies, and says when something is unknown.

Jules doesn’t buy anything for you or store your card details.

How much does it cost?

Jules is free during early access (as of October 2026). If you buy something, you pay the store directly at the store’s price.

Does Jules work on Android?

Not yet. As of October 2026, Jules works only in iMessage, so you need an iPhone.

Which stores does Jules search?

Jules isn’t limited to a fixed list of partner stores. For each request it searches the web and Google Shopping, then reads the store’s own product page to check the item, price and sizes. It looks at the brand’s own site and established fashion retailers first, then other stores. Resale marketplaces such as eBay, Poshmark and Grailed come in when you’re open to pre-owned or nothing new turns up. If Jules can’t read a store’s page, it tells you to check the price and sizes there rather than guessing.

Is my data private?

Jules uses what you share only to run Jules for you. We never sell it, use it for ads, or share it for other companies’ marketing. Connecting Gmail is optional and read-only, and you can reply STOP any time to stop texts. The Privacy policy has the details.

Should I use Jules, Phia, Google Lens or ChatGPT?

It depends on what you’re doing. As of October 2026:

  • Google Lens is quicker when you just want to know what something is, or to see lookalikes from a photo.
  • Phia suits shopping in a browser: its extension and iPhone app compare new and resale prices on the page you’re already looking at.
  • ChatGPT’s shopping research suits researching a category, like which laptop to buy.
  • Jules suits you if you’d rather text a screenshot and get the item back in your size, with cheaper and resale options, and have Jules remember your sizes and keep watching the price.

How do I start?

Request early access on the sign-up page with your iPhone number. You must be 18 or older. Early access is invite-only for now; once you’re in, you text Jules from your iPhone.
