How signup information is used

Jules stores your name, birthday, gender selection, the number you enter, the consent disclosure, its version, and the time you submit it in our private Supabase database. We use your name to personalize your account and your phone number to contact you. Birthday and gender are saved as account details; they do not establish sizes or shopping preferences. We also store a protected identifier for your connection to limit repeated submissions. Signup information is not sold or shared for other companies’ marketing.

When you start chatting, Photon delivers messages, Supabase stores conversation history, and OpenAI processes shopping requests and images. Please share only information you want Jules to use for shopping.

Invited testers may receive an onboarding text after signup. Gmail is optional and requires separate Google permission and receipt-analysis consent. Follow-up questions and corrections happen in your text conversation with Jules.

This signup does not verify ownership of your number.
