How signup information is used

Jules stores the number you enter, the consent disclosure, its version, and the time you submit it in our private Supabase database. We use this information to manage early access and messaging consent. We also store a protected identifier for your connection to limit repeated submissions. Signup information is not sold or shared for other companies’ marketing.

When you start chatting, Photon delivers messages, Supabase stores conversation history, and OpenAI processes shopping requests and images. Please share only information you want Jules to use for shopping.

Before outbound signup messages are enabled, Jules will provide published messaging terms and a verified way to withdraw consent.
