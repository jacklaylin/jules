# Jules sourcing registry

Starter list: 20 curated fashion retailers and 100 brands, excluding fast fashion and big-box general merchandise stores. This is an editorial sourcing list, not a sales ranking. Retailer numbers give the default priority; brands are grouped for readability.

Search order: registered official brand sites → registered trusted retailers → broader web retrieval. Search uses domain filters, followed by independent product-page checks. Registered sites may be blocked, lack structured product data, or have no online shop. Identity, requested variant and eligibility precede store ranking. Registration does not establish stock, size, price, shipping, authenticity of an individual item, or retailer policy.

## Maintenance and verification

The executable source is `lib/source-registry.js`. Domain identity was reviewed against brand/retailer primary websites on October 8, 2026, with search fallback for blocked homepages. Some sites are corporate/identity references rather than direct shops (for example Alden and Comme des Garçons); retailer retrieval remains necessary. Each sourced product still needs its own live page check. Geographic redirects outside registered domains remain rejected. Goldwin and Supreme use their current destinations, goldwin.global and supreme.com.

The registry is shared by retrieval filters, listing verification permission and merchant ranking. Add a source after checking its own primary website; do not guess a domain or product URL. Unknown retailers remain unreviewed. Expanding web retrieval does not automatically authorize fetching an unknown retailer. Existing Foot Locker verification permission is retained for previously recorded links, outside this starter priority list.

The domain-filter implementation follows [official OpenAI web-search documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/tools-web-search#domain-filtering).

For unnamed images, Jules still needs visual identification first; once it has readable product text or a supported identity, merchant sourcing uses the registry. The three search passes share a 95-second network deadline. A blocked site is a failed check, not evidence that a product does not exist. No catalog scraper or store-specific API integration was added.
